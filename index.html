About Marwadi University

Marwadi University is one of Gujarat's leading private universities, located in Rajkot. It offers quality education in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Computer Applications, Science, Law, Commerce, Design, and many other disciplines. The university focuses on academic excellence, innovation, research, and industry collaboration.

Vision

To become a globally recognized university by providing quality education, promoting innovation, and preparing students to become responsible professionals and leaders.

Mission

  • Provide quality education with practical learning.
  • Encourage innovation and research.
  • Develop leadership and entrepreneurship skills.
  • Promote ethical values and social responsibility.
  • Build strong industry collaborations.
